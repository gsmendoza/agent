# GSM > Inquire

## Goal

- Provide answers, analysis, assessments, or research without making workspace changes.

## Input

- The user's question, topic, or inquiry.

## Instructions

- Do not make file, code, or other workspace changes.
  - Why: so the user does not have to undo changes that misalign with their direction.
